# Colombia department map data

The department boundaries in `colombia-departments.svg` are based on the simplified Colombia ADM1 administrative boundary dataset published by geoBoundaries:

- Dataset: [geoBoundaries Colombia ADM1](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/COL/ADM1/)
- Geometry source: OpenStreetMap and Wambacher
- Dataset license: [Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0 (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/)
- Dataset build: December 12, 2023; source data update: January 19, 2023
- Geometry download: [simplified GeoJSON](https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/raw/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/COL/ADM1/geoBoundaries-COL-ADM1_simplified.geojson)

The SVG is a display rendering of the 33 ADM1 units (32 departments and Bogotá Capital District). Department names and example principal cities are presented separately in `../data/coverage-departments.json`. The city list is illustrative, is not a municipal boundary dataset, and does not limit the stated delivery coverage to those cities.

The derived department geometry in `colombia-departments.svg` is distributed under ODbL 1.0. Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors / geoBoundaries.
